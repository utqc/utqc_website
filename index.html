The University of Toronto Quantum Computing Club is an undergraduate-led community dedicated to making quantum computing accessible, exciting, and hands-on for students from all backgrounds.

We run lecture series, workshops, and tutorials that introduce quantum computing from the ground up. We also have research projects and host industry events and reseach collabs! Whether you’re new to quantum or ready to dive deeper, there’s a place to learn, grow, and prepare for careers in cutting-edge technology.
