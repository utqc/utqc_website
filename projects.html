Projects

UTQC projects give students the opportunity to apply quantum computing concepts to real-world problems and collaborate with researchers, organizations, and industry.






Current Projects

Quantum PacMan Challange

In collaboration with the University of Toronto Robotics Association, this design project is focused on creating a quantum-based algorithm that can effectively play the popular arcade game PacMan. By encoding the game state into a Hamiltonian state, we can pick optimal player paths using a quantum walk approach. Preprint coming soon.

See #nv-diamond-project in Discord for more info.

NV-diamond Sensing

Supported by the SPIE Outreach Grant under the NAQC Photonics Foundry alongside Quantum Village, this project provided students with hands-on experience designing and building a quantum sensor. By using Nitrogen-Vacancy centers in diamonds, these devices are able to detect microscale magnetic fields. Paper under review.

See #quantum-pacbots in Discord for more info.
