Recurring Events

Workshop

qLearn

Running since 2023, these are weekly 1-hour sessions run by one of UTQC's upper year academic members on quantum computing foundations. All majors and years welcome, no prerequisties required! Every year, two different series covered: quantum hardware and quantum algorithms. Sometimes, there are special collabs where you can learn from actual research scientists in the industry. Eg, LG Electronics AI Research Lab of Toronto.

Hackathon

IBM Qiskit Fall Fest

A unique beginner friendly quantum computing hackathon, and series of speaker topics that covers quantum computing and frameworks for quantum computing, hosted by UTQC with the support of IBM! The main goal is to expose students to the quantum world and allow them to experience all the fun that the world of tech offers. Expect techincal workshops, tutorial sessions, activities and challenges between quantum and classical computing, coding with a real quantum computer, and speaker sessions!

Annual Conference

Toronto Q-SITE Conference

UTQC's annual conference that brings together students and industry professionals, aka Canada's largest quantum technologies conference for undergraduate and early-graduate students. Guests come from across Canada and US to talk about their research in different areas of quantum, and available opportunities at their institutes. Also hosts hackathons! Find out more here.

Lecture Series

QIS

The Quantum Introductory Symposium program is a series of talks about big ideas in quantum computing. Designed to be accessible for all audiences, each session is hosted by a Dr/Prof/Grad Student who share their research experience in quantum computing, from Quantum Optics to Error Correction and Quantum Algorithms. Speakers include Dr. Thi Ha Kyaw, Prof. Aephraim Steinberg, and more!

Networking

QuiP

Panel Discussion about internships and research oppurtunities with industry experts representing key companies in quantum computing! Guest Speakers come from Centre for Quantum Information and Quantum Control (CQIQC) @ UofT, Quantrop, Xanadu, MatterLab, Zapata, Institute for Quantum Computing @ UW, Stewart Blusson Quantum Matter Institute (SBQMI) @ UBC, and more!
